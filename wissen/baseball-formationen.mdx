## Depth Chart (formation_id: 58)

Baseball verwendet einen einzigen Depth Chart. Die zwölf Positionen decken Pitcher, Infield, Outfield und den Designated Hitter ab.

| pos_index | position_id | Kürzel | position_name |
|-----------|------------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | 46 | SP | Starting Pitcher |
| 2 | 47 | RP | Relief Pitcher |
| 3 | 48 | CL | Closer |
| 4 | 37 | C | Catcher |
| 5 | 38 | 1B | 1st Base |
| 6 | 39 | 2B | 2nd Base |
| 7 | 40 | 3B | 3rd Base |
| 8 | 41 | SS | Shortstop |
| 9 | 43 | LF | Left Field |
| 10 | 44 | CF | Center Field |
| 11 | 42 | RF | Right Field |
| 12 | 45 | DH | Designated Hitter |

## Positionsbeschreibungen

### Pitcher
| Kürzel | Rolle |
|--------|-------|
| SP | Startwurf, wirft typisch 5–7 Innings |
| RP | Ablösepitcher, übernimmt im Spielverlauf |
| CL | Closer, sichert Führung im letzten Inning |

### Infield
| Kürzel | Rolle |
|--------|-------|
| C | Catcher, hinter dem Home Plate |
| 1B | First Baseman |
| 2B | Second Baseman |
| 3B | Third Baseman |
| SS | Shortstop, zwischen 2nd und 3rd Base |

### Outfield & Designated
| Kürzel | Rolle |
|--------|-------|
| LF | Left Fielder |
| CF | Center Fielder, läuft die meisten Fläche ab |
| RF | Right Fielder |
| DH | Designated Hitter, schlägt statt Pitcher (kein Feld) |

<Note>
  Baseball-Formationen haben keine xpos/ypos-Koordinaten (alle `0.0`). Der Depth Chart bildet Positionshierarchie ab, nicht Feldpositionen.
</Note>

## DB-Abfrage

```sql
SELECT
    t.id        AS formation_id,
    t.name      AS formation,
    t.fullname,
    tp.pos_index,
    p.id        AS position_id,
    p.shortname AS position,
    p.name      AS position_name
FROM db127096_17.tactical_position tp
LEFT JOIN db127096_17.tactical_formation t ON tp.tactical_formation_id = t.id
LEFT JOIN db127096_17.position p           ON p.id = tp.position_id
WHERE t.sport_id = 3;
```
