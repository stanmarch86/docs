## Defensive Schemes

### 4-4 Defense (formation_id: 68)

Vier Defensive Linemen + vier Linebacker, gut gegen den Laufangriff.

| pos_index | Kürzel | Anzahl |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| 1–4 | DL | Defensive Lineman (4×) |
| 5–8 | LB | Linebacker (4×) |
| 9 | LCB | Left Cornerback |
| 10 | SS | Strong Safety |
| 11 | FS | Free Safety |
| 12 | RCB | Right Cornerback |
| 13 | NB | Nickel Back |

### 4-3 Defense (formation_id: 71)

Vier Defensive Linemen + drei Linebacker, verbreitetes Basisschema.

| pos_index | Kürzel | Anzahl |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| 1–4 | DL | Defensive Lineman (4×) |
| 5–7 | LB | Linebacker (3×) |
| 8 | NB | Nickel Back |
| 9 | SS | Strong Safety |
| 10 | LCB | Left Cornerback |
| 11 | FS | Free Safety |
| 12 | RCB | Right Cornerback |

### 3-4 Defense (formation_id: 74)

Drei Defensive Linemen + vier Linebacker, flexibler gegen Pass und Lauf.

| pos_index | Kürzel | Anzahl |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| 1–3 | DL | Defensive Lineman (3×) |
| 4–7 | LB | Linebacker (4×) |
| 8 | NB | Nickel Back |
| 9 | SS | Strong Safety |
| 10 | RCB | Right Cornerback |
| 11 | LCB | Left Cornerback |
| 12 | FS | Free Safety |

<Note>
  American Football Formations haben keine xpos/ypos-Koordinaten (alle `0.0`). Die Schemas (4-4, 4-3, 3-4) beschreiben die Anzahl der Defensivspieler pro Reihe (D-Line / Linebacker), nicht die exakten Feldpositionen.
</Note>

## DB-Abfrage

```sql
SELECT
    t.id        AS formation_id,
    t.name      AS formation,
    t.fullname,
    tp.pos_index,
    p.id        AS position_id,
    p.shortname AS position,
    p.name      AS position_name
FROM db127096_17.tactical_position tp
LEFT JOIN db127096_17.tactical_formation t ON tp.tactical_formation_id = t.id
LEFT JOIN db127096_17.position p           ON p.id = tp.position_id
WHERE t.sport_id = 16;
```
